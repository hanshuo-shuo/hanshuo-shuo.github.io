selected publications

  1. ICML
    Of Mice and Machines: A Comparison of Learning Between Real World Mice and RL Agents
    Shuo Han, German Espinosa, Junda Huang, Daniel A. Dombeck, Malcolm A. MacIver, and Bradly C. Stadie
    Figure
    In International Conference on Machine Learning, 2025
    First author.

    Combines behavioral data analysis and PyTorch modeling to compare learning in mice and artificial agents. Uses trajectory analysis and uncertainty quantification to guide reinforcement learning (RL), using variance across action values as an uncertainty signal that encourages cautious decisions. Also evaluates a VLM/LLM (GPT-4o) agent in the same task.

    Abs HTML

    A behavioral-data study of biological mice, RL agents, and a vision-language LLM agent (GPT-4o) in a shared predator-avoidance environment. Trajectory analysis reveals contrasting risk-taking strategies, motivating two biology-inspired learning mechanisms. These include uncertainty quantification using variance across action values to inform a variance-penalized temporal-difference learning objective. A focused VLM/LLM experiment examines how visual observations and task instructions translate into closed-loop decisions.

  2. TMLR
    D2 Actor Critic: Diffusion Actor Meets Distributional Critic
    Lunjun Zhang*, Shuo Han*, Hanrui Lyu, and Bradly C. Stadie
    Figure
    Transactions on Machine Learning Research, 2025
    * Equal contribution.

    A model-free actor-critic algorithm that learns expressive diffusion policies online for continuous control. Low-variance supervision at each denoising step supports stable policy improvement without backpropagation through the full diffusion chain. A distributional critic combines categorical return modeling with clipped double Q-learning to address return uncertainty and value-estimation bias.

    Abs HTML

    A model-free actor-critic method that learns expressive diffusion policies online. Stable policy improvement and a distributional critic support effective learning across challenging continuous-control tasks.

  3. IJERPH
    Evaluating the Effect of Government Emission Reduction Policy: Evidence from Demonstration Cities in China
    Yunchan Zhu, Shuo Han, Yimeng Zhang, and Qi Huang
    Figure
    International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health, 2021

    Applies causal inference to measure policy impact from city-level panel data (2003–2016), using Difference-in-Differences (DiD) with city and year fixed effects. Assesses treatment-effect estimates with event-study pre-trend checks, 500 randomized placebo tests, and sensitivity analyses across outcome definitions and samples. The study reports average reductions of 23.8% in industrial SO2 emissions and 17.5% in industrial wastewater emissions.

    Abs DOI HTML

    A quasi-experimental evaluation of China’s energy-conservation and emission-reduction fiscal policy using city-level panel data from 2003 to 2016. A multi-period Difference-in-Differences model estimates policy effects, with event-study diagnostics, randomized placebo tests, and alternative outcome and sample specifications assessing robustness. Mechanism analyses examine changes in industrial structure and local fiscal capacity.

working papers

Beyond Observed Rescue: Evaluating Repeatable Intervention Value in VLA Policies

Working paper · 2026

When a recovery branch succeeds, is the intervention worth repeating? This work studies vision-language-action policies through saved-state branching, repeated rollouts, and separate selection and evaluation. It distinguishes deadline-sensitive acceleration, repeatable task rescue, and the limits of transferring intervention benefits to fresh robot states.

When Does Oracle Decoder Evaluation Predict Learned-Input Value?

Working paper · 2026

A decoder that improves exact spatial targets may behave differently on learned predictions. This work separates changes in baseline scores from changes in decoder corrections, and examines their effects on region-screening decisions. Controlled synthetic studies and sequence-held-out real RGB evaluation connect reconstruction quality, acceptance thresholds, and downstream decision value.
